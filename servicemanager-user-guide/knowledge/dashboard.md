# Analytics

Knowledge analytics provides information across all knowledge bases. This information can help you determine the usefulness or popularity of a particular knowledge base compared with others.

## Available charts

The following charts are available to help you analyze your Knowledge data. Use the Next and Previous arrows at the bottom to scroll through the charts.

![Chart Next and Previous](/_books/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/images/dashboard-next-previous.png)

### Articles Per Knowledge Base

Use this chart to compare the size of each knowledge base by number of articles. Filter the chart by article status.

![Dashboard Articles Per Knowledge Base](/_books/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/images/dashboard-articles-per-knowledge-base.png)

### Top 10 Most Viewed Articles

See which articles have been viewed the most. Filter the chart by time and source. Click any entry to go directly to the article.

![Dashboard 10 Most Viewed Articles](/_books/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/images/dashboard-articles-most-viewed.png)

### Top 10 Most Liked Articles

See which articles are the most popular. Filter the chart by time and source. Click any entry to go directly to the article.

![Dashboard Top 10 Liked Articles](/_books/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/images/dashboard-articles-most-liked.png)

### Top 10 Most Searched Terms

See which terms are searched for most often. Filter the chart by time and source.

![Dashboard Most Searched Terms](/_books/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/images/dashboard-most-searched-terms.png)

### Top 10 Most Searched Terms With No Results

See which of the most frequently searched terms return no results. Filter the chart by time and source.

![Dashboard Most Searched Terms with no results](/_books/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/images/dashboard-most-searched-terms-with-no-results.png)
