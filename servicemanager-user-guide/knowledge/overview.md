# Overview

The **Overview** is a landing page for knowledge managers and contributors that displays a grid of cards, each listing up to three items with links to see more. Knowledge managers (Knowledge Managers and Administrators) get one set of cards and contributors get another. Each card only shows to users with the rights it needs.

![Overview for a knowledge contributor](/_books/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/images/overview-contributor.png)

## Before you begin

* You must have the **Knowledge Administrator**, **Knowledge Manager**, or **KnowledgeContributor** role to access the Overview.
* If you have more than one of the roles listed above, you will only see the cards for the highest role you have. For example, if you are a Knowledge Manager and a Knowledge Contributor, you will only see the Knowledge Manager cards.

|Card|Who|Each item shows|Links and buttons|
|--|--|--|--|
|Pending my approval|Knowledge managers|Article title, how many days it has waited (colored by age: 0–1 days: green, 2–4 days: yellow, 5–9 days: orange, 10+ days: red), author and knowledge base. Oldest first|View all pending approvals (only when some are waiting)|
|Due for review|Knowledge managers|Article title, knowledge base, reviewer and due date. Soonest first.|Opens the article's Reviews tab|
|My reviews|Contributors|Article title, knowledge base and due date. Soonest first.|Opens the article's Reviews tab|
|Latest articles|Knowledge managers|Article title, knowledge base, status, author and date created. Newest first|New Article|
|My articles|Contributors|Article title, status and knowledge base. Most recently updated first|New Article|
|Knowledge Bases|Knowledge Administrator|Name, number of articles and when last updated. Most recently updated first|Knowledge managers: View all knowledge bases, New Knowledge Base|
|My knowledge bases|Knowledge Managers, Contributors|Name, number of articles and when last updated. Most recently updated first|View my knowledge bases|
|Most recently updated articles|Contributors|Article title, knowledge base, author and when last updated|Browse articles|
|Analytics|Knowledge Managers|Chart: "Most viewed articles (last 7 days)"|View more analytics|
