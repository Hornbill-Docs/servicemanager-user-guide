# Approvals

Approvals are used to manage the lifecycle of knowledge articles, ensuring that changes are reviewed and approved before they are published. This helps maintain the quality and accuracy of the knowledge base.

This view offers a list of all knowledge articles that are pending approval, across all of the knowledge bases, along with their current status and the assigned approvers. Users can filter the list by various criteria, such as article type, status, or assigned approver.

Approvals can be actioned either individually or in bulk. When an article is approved, it moves to the next stage in the workflow, while rejected articles are sent back to the author for revisions.
