# Knowledge Bases

This view provides a list of all knowledge bases that you own or can contribute to. You can view the details of each knowledge base, including its name, description, and the number of articles it contains. You can also create new knowledge bases.

![Knowledge Base List](/_books/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/images/knowledge-base-list.png)

Each knowledge base is displayed in a row, with the knowledge base name, the number of articles, and when the knowledge base was last updated. You can click the knowledge base name to view its details, including the list of articles it contains.

## Breadcrumb

![Breadcrumb](/_books/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/images/manage-knowledge-base-breadcrumb.png)

A breadcrumb is shown at the top of the page, with the *Knowledge* link that takes you to the [Knowledge Overview](/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/overview). A back button is also shown, which returns you to the previous page that you were viewing.

## Status filter

* **Active and in Development**: This filter shows all knowledge bases that are either active or in development. Active knowledge bases are those that are currently being used and have articles available for viewing. Knowledge bases in development are those that are being created or modified and may not yet have articles available.
* **All**: This filter shows all knowledge bases, regardless of their status. This includes active, in development, and retired knowledge bases. Retired knowledge bases are those that are no longer being used and may have been archived or deleted.
* **In development**: This filter shows only knowledge bases that are currently in development. These knowledge bases may not yet have articles available for viewing and may be undergoing changes or updates.
* **Active**: This filter shows only knowledge bases that are currently active and have articles available for viewing. Active knowledge bases are those that are being used and maintained, and may have a variety of articles on different topics.
* **Retired**: This filter shows only knowledge bases that have been retired and are no longer being used.

## New knowledge base

![New Knowledge Base button](/_books/servicemanager-user-guide/knowledge/images/new-knowledge-base-button.png)

The **New Knowledge Base** button opens the new knowledge base page, where you can create a new knowledge base. You will be prompted to enter the name and description of the knowledge base, as well as select its status (active, in development, or retired). Once created, the new knowledge base will be added to the list and you can start adding articles to it.
